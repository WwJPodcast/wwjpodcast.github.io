About

The Practice

Fifteen minutes in the Gospels, five days a week. In one month, it’s a habit. In three, you notice the difference. In six, you’re asking your family to try it. Give it a year and His words come to mind right when you need them.

We take vitamins, exercise, sleep, and brush our teeth every day, because the results come from keeping at it. The soul is no different. The Gospels are the story all of Scripture points to or stands on, so they’re the best place to let Jesus change us.

This doesn’t replace the rest of your Bible, or your church. It makes room for Jesus. “If a man love me, he will keep my words: and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him.” (John 14:23)

Read the Gospels or listen, but try it. Fifteen minutes a day. Jesus will do the rest.

FAQ
How do I add this to my home screen?

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Computer: bookmark this page (Ctrl+D, or Cmd+D on a Mac).

It then opens like an app, with its own icon.

Will it remember where I stopped?

Yes. On this device it keeps your place in every episode, your listening speed, and the last episode you played in each harmony.

If you clear your browser data or switch to another device, you will start fresh.

I already know the story of Jesus’ life. Why listen again?

You don’t stop spending time with someone because you already know their story. This isn’t about new information; it’s about time in His company, day after day.

Hearing familiar passages in order, and read aloud, also tends to bring out details we’ve skimmed past.

Why the King James Version?

The KJV is in the public domain, so the text can be read freely without licensing limits. Its rhythm was also shaped for being read aloud, which suits listening.

Many listeners grew up with it, and many are meeting it for the first time. Either way, you hear the Gospels as they have been spoken for centuries.

Why Monday through Friday, not daily?

Walking with Jesus is built to be a habit you can keep. Five days gives you a steady rhythm without the pressure of a never-miss-a-day streak.

Weekends are left open for church, family, rest, and catching up on anything you missed. Every episode stays available, so you can go at your own pace.

Who is reading? Are the voices real?

The voices are AI-generated, and we say so plainly. The words are not generated: every passage is the King James text, read in full.
