About

What is Walking with Jesus?

An effort to teach the value of daily time with Jesus through His words in the Gospels, and to make it an easy habit to adopt.

Time with Jesus is what changes a life, and the Gospels are where we find Him: His words, His actions, and ultimately His sacrifice. The value isn’t in making it through the Gospels once, or in hearing something new. It’s in regular, prolonged exposure to the most important person in the Bible, day after day, as He shapes who we are.

FAQ

Questions people ask

I already know the story of Jesus' life. Why listen again?

You don’t stop spending time with someone because you already know their story. This isn’t about new information; it’s about time in His company, day after day.

Hearing familiar passages in order, and read aloud, also tends to bring out details we’ve skimmed past.

Why the King James Version?

The KJV is in the public domain, so the text can be read freely without licensing limits. Its rhythm was also shaped for being read aloud, which suits listening.

Many listeners grew up with it, and many are meeting it for the first time. Either way, you hear the Gospels as they have been spoken for centuries.

Why Monday through Friday, not daily?

Walking with Jesus is built to be a habit you can keep. Five days gives you a steady rhythm without the pressure of a never-miss-a-day streak.

Weekends are left open for church, family, rest, and catching up on anything you missed. Every episode stays available, so you can go at your own pace.

How did this come to be?

For over thirty years I sat in the pews and called myself a follower of Christ, yet too much of God's Word remained a closed book to me. When I shifted from reading to listening, everything changed. "My sheep hear my voice" (John 10:27), and "faith cometh by hearing" (Romans 10:17).

But the more I listened, the more I wanted Jesus Himself: His words, His miracles, His time with broken people, every single day.

So I built the thing I was looking for: a daily Gospel podcast and YouTube channel, in the KJV, short enough to fit into any day.

Why not just read my Bible?

You should! This isn't a replacement for your Bible; it's a way to build the habit of showing up. Many people find that hearing the Gospels read aloud helps them notice things they've read past a dozen times.

Listening also fits places reading doesn't: driving, walking, folding laundry, or a tired evening. Let this open the door, then open your Bible and go deeper.

Why not an audiobook Bible?

You can! It's a great way to hear the Gospels and Jesus' words.

Here are a few things to consider. An audiobook Bible reads straight through, even in the Gospels, so the same events are retold four times in four separate places. Walking with Jesus is a harmony: Matthew, Mark, Luke and John blended into a single chronological story of Jesus' life.

There are many free and paid audiobook Bibles to download. The Walking with Jesus podcast and YouTube playlist are free.

Who is reading? Are the voices real?

The voices are AI-generated, and we say so plainly. The words are not generated: every passage is the King James text, read in full.
